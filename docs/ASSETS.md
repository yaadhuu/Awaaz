# Audio assets

All radio-noise beds used by the web app (Link lab, Library) and by `scripts/make_channel_samples.py`. Rule from the brief: only CC0, public domain or CC BY; no NC, ND or unknown licences; no speech from real people. The full record (source URL, author, licence and URL, date accessed, sha256, duration, voiced-frame share, waveform peaks) is in `assets/manifest.json`. Files: `assets/beds/*.wav` (16 kHz mono, at most 30 s and 1 MB) and `web/public/assets/beds/*.mp3` (48 kbps for the web).

## How they were chosen

- `scripts/fetch_radio_beds.py` searched Wikimedia Commons (8 queries: radio static, shortwave noise, white noise radio, radio interference, morse code, squelch, radio tuning, static noise) and the Internet Archive (3 queries). It downloads at most the first 4 MB of a file and cuts 30 s with ffmpeg.
- 130 candidates were looked at: 2 kept, 128 rejected. Reasons are in `assets/manifest.json` (`rejected`). The largest groups: licence not allowed (43, almost all CC BY-SA), title or description suggesting speech, music or a pronunciation recording, too short (under 5 s), or download/decode failures.
- A voice-activity check (webrtcvad, mode 3) rejects any file with more than 50 % voiced frames, as a guard against speech. It is a heuristic; nobody has listened to every file yet (Open question).
- Because fewer than 6 downloads passed, six beds were generated by our own script (CC0, seeded). They are simple models of real channel effects and are labelled "Generated by TinyTalk" everywhere.

## Channel samples

`scripts/make_channel_samples.py` mixes every bed onto the first test message of each language (cached synthetic Timbre speech, voice Vasudha) at 20, 10, 5 and 0 dB SNR with seed 2026: 96 files in `assets/channel/` (not committed; regenerate with the script) and `assets/channel/index.json`. No speech-to-text has been run on them: that needs the credit estimate approved first.

## Not used

- CC BY-SA recordings (many radio-noise files on Commons) were refused by the brief's licence rule. Whether to allow share-alike is listed as an Open question.
- No real broadcast voices were used.
