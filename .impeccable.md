# Design Context

Audience: first-time public visitors arriving from LinkedIn, Indian multilingual users, and Gnani hackathon evaluators inspecting the engineering.

Use cases: understand adaptive voice communication in one continuous message journey; listen to saved examples; adjust simulated packet loss; compare audio, transcript and fact payloads; enter the existing demo; inspect measured evidence separately.

Brand: Awaaz. Tagline: Every voice deserves to reach. Warm, literary, artistic, classy and distinctly Indian. Ivory paper, forest ink, terracotta; expressive Fraunces headings with restrained Anek body typography. The human message is the memorable visual anchor. No copied brands or site content, neon/glass UI, card grids, bounce, overshoot, scroll hijacking, or inflated claims.

Constraints: hackathon deadline; existing React/Motion stack plus Lenis requested by the user; mobile and desktop, touch and keyboard, native touch scroll and reduced-motion support. The landing uses saved evidence without spending credits; the explicitly enabled voice workspace offers four capped live model requests per visitor per day. Real radio recordings are separately credited references. All ten speech languages are selectable; the older measured fact benchmark covers three. No translation or real-radio field-validation claim. Recording is live capture followed by REST transcription after stopping, not streaming partial captions.

QA order: audit, critique, polish. Final user report: 10 lines. Quality goal: a finished editorial flagship landing page with a reliable route into the prototype.
